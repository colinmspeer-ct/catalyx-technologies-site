Research and development

Photocatalytic oxidation for water treatment

A research program. Not a product, and not a claim of removal performance.

The objectives below are goals, not results. Catalyx does not offer a water-treatment product and does not publish removal rates, by-product data, or energy figures for water.

The opportunity

Advanced oxidation processes are established in water treatment, but most rely on continuously dosed chemicals (ozone, hydrogen peroxide) or energy-intensive UV systems. A durable, immobilized photocatalyst that generates hydroxyl radicals in place could reduce chemical handling and consumables. Catalyx has already solved durability and maintainability for PCO in air. The water program asks whether that engineering can carry over to water.

The air work is the commercial FRESH+™ PCO+UV line. The chemistry is described on the technology page.

Program objectives

  1. Define target contaminants and water matrices. The specific contaminants and matrices are not listed until they are confirmed.
  2. Design and build a bench-scale photocatalytic reactor with an immobilized catalyst.
  3. Measure contaminant removal, by-product formation, catalyst stability, and energy per order of removal against a conventional baseline.
  4. Identify the scale-up path and pilot partners.
