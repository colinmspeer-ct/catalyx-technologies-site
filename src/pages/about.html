Catalyx Technologies LLC

Engineers who commercialized photocatalytic oxidation

Catalyx Technologies LLC is an engineering company focused on photocatalytic oxidation. Through its Fresh Plus International division (DBA), Catalyx designs and sells FRESH+™ PCO+UV air purification systems for produce and cold-chain storage. The Fresh Plus International site reports more than 20 years of work with PCO, and Catalyx holds US and international patents on the FRESH+™ design.

Product specifications and support stay on the Fresh Plus International site. The Catalyx products page is the family overview. Water treatment is a research program, not a second product line.

Beyond produce

PCO+UV also applies to odor control (animal health, residential living facilities) and VOC control (aldehydes, anesthesia gases, solvents, adhesives). Catalyx supports projects that explore these uses.
