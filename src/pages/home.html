How photocatalytic oxidation works

Nothing is added to the air or water stream, and the catalyst isn't used up in the reaction.

  1. UV light activates the catalyst UV light activates a photocatalyst surface.
  2. Hydroxyl radicals form That surface continuously generates hydroxyl radicals (•OH), among the strongest oxidants used in treatment.
  3. Contaminants are oxidized Organic compounds that contact the surface are oxidized, ideally all the way to carbon dioxide and water.
